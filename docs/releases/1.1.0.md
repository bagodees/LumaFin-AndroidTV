# LumaFin 1.1.0

LumaFin has a new look for your TV.

- **A new Home screen:** browse your rows with a large preview area and title logos.
- **Your libraries in the side menu:** jump straight to each library from across the app.
- **New details pages:** title logos, pill-shaped buttons and season tabs make browsing movies and shows easier.
- **Tabbed Movies and TV pages:** explore Recommended, Library, Collections or Studios, and Genres, with picture tiles for genres and studios.
- **A restyled Live TV guide:** the guide now fits the LumaFin look and includes the side menu.

Prefer the old look? Go to **Settings → Appearance & Home → Home → Switch to classic Jellyfin style**.
