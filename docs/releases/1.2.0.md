# LumaFin 1.2.0

LumaFin now looks the same on every screen, and there's more you can set up your way.

- **Request movies and shows:** with the SeerrFin plugin on your server, Home shows what's trending and coming soon, plus your own requests and where each one stands. Every title opens its own page, just like the ones in your library, with the cast, a trailer and similar titles. Request it there, choose which seasons you want, or request the seasons a show you have, or have already asked for in part, is missing.
- **New settings:** a full-screen layout with a category menu and Back to Home at the bottom. Each category says what's inside, Appearance is split into smaller pages, every choice shows its current setting, and About shows your version and whether an update is out.
- **Make Home yours:**
  - Choose the top bar style.
  - Pick landscape or portrait cards.
  - Pin genres, studios, playlists and Favorites.
  - Remove shows from Up Next.
  - Choose which libraries appear in the side menu.
- **Your server's Home layout:** if your server uses the Home Screen Sections plugin, LumaFin can show its rows.
- **One look everywhere:** cards, spacing, row titles and the clock now match across Home, libraries, details pages, actor pages and search. Cards show the year and rating.
- **Better details:** episode pages show the show's main cast as well as guest stars. Top Rated favours well-reviewed titles. Titles with only a poster show it in full.
- **Fixes:**
  - Home no longer jumps back to the top while you browse.
  - Titles you're watching again no longer show a watched check.
  - Search opens on a clean background.
  - Subtitles are shown above the player controls.
  - Movies and episodes start faster, right at your resume point.
  - Dolby audio starts reliably through soundbars and receivers, and your audio and subtitle choices stick.
  - Video no longer stays frozen after a big skip.
  - Less memory use during playback, so LumaFin is steadier on 2 GB streaming sticks.
  - Pressing Back on a details page after watching no longer closes the app.
  - Fixed a crash when a phone sends a title while another is playing.
