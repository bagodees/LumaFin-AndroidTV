# Android TV tester recruitment draft

Title: Looking for Android TV / Google TV testers for LumaFin, a Jellyfin client

Hi everyone! I'm building LumaFin, a Jellyfin client for Android TV and Google TV, and I'm looking for people to help test it before its first Google Play release.

It connects to your own Jellyfin server and focuses on remote-friendly browsing, customizable Home rows, and playback controls. If you don't have a Jellyfin server, I can provide access to a demo server with sample movies and episodes.

I'm looking for at least 12 testers who can stay opted into the closed test for 14 consecutive days and use the app during that time. Feedback on navigation, playback, subtitles, readability, and anything that freezes or crashes would be especially helpful.

You'll need an Android TV / Google TV device, a Google account, and a US Google Play country for this initial test. This test is for adults 18+. LumaFin is a TV app; a phone-only installation won't test its remote controls and TV layouts.

If you're interested, comment or send me a private message and I'll share the joining instructions. Please don't post your email address or server credentials publicly.

Thanks for helping me get it ready!

## Before posting

- Confirm the closed release is available to invited testers before sending installation links.
- Add testers to the track's email list, or create a Google Group and add its address to the track. A group lets people join without sharing their address in a public comment.
- Joining order: join the approved tester list/group, opt in, then install on the TV using the same Google account.
- Opt-in: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/io.github.bagodees.lumafin
- Store: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.github.bagodees.lumafin
- Share demo credentials privately. Testers may use their own Jellyfin servers instead.
- Check the community's posting rules before publishing. This file is a draft, not a published post.
